# Welcome to Clarify 👋

Clarify is your personal, AI-powered productivity assistant. It combines the simplicity of a beautiful to-do list with the intelligence of an advanced AI. 

Whether you need to quickly jot down a thought, organize a massive project, or ask questions about your past notes, Clarify is designed to be fast, reliable, and entirely private.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 📝 Notes & ✅ Tasks
Easily capture what matters. Create detailed notes for your thoughts or actionable tasks to keep your day organized. You can categorize tasks, set priorities, and mark them complete with a satisfying swipe.

### 🤖 AI Assistant
Think of Clarify as a personal assistant that has read all your notes. You can chat with the AI directly inside the app to summarize your ideas, find forgotten tasks, or get insights based *only* on your personal data.

### ⚡️ Task Breakdown
Overwhelmed by a big goal? Just type it into the "Breakdown" tool. The AI will instantly slice it into small, manageable, bite-sized tasks and add them directly to your Inbox.

### ☁️ Seamless Sync & 📱 Offline Support
Whether you're on a subway or deep in the woods without internet, Clarify works flawlessly. It saves everything locally to your device first. The moment you reconnect to the internet, it silently syncs everything to the cloud. You never have to wait for a loading spinner.

### 🔍 Smart Search
Instantly find anything you've ever written. Clarify's search is incredibly fast and searches across all your notes and tasks simultaneously.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Open the App**: Launch Clarify on your device.
2. **Sign In or Try as Guest**: You can securely sign in using your Google Account to enable cloud sync. If you just want to test it out, select "Continue as Guest" to use it completely offline!
3. **Capture a Thought**: Tap the big purple **"+"** button at the bottom right of the screen to quickly create a Note or a Task.
4. **Organize**: Swipe left or right on your tasks in the Inbox to complete or delete them quickly.
5. **Ask AI**: Tap the Sparkle (✨) icon to open the AI Assistant and ask it a question about your day!

---

## 🧠 Using the AI

Clarify's AI is built specifically around **your** data. It doesn't give you generic answers; it gives you answers based on what you've written.

- **Ask AI**: Try asking questions like *"What are my high priority tasks for today?"* or *"Summarize my notes about the kitchen renovation."*
- **Break Down Task**: Go to the AI tab and select "Breakdown". Type a massive goal like *"Launch a podcast"* and watch it generate 10 simple steps you can add to your tasks with one click.
- **Dashboard Insights**: Every time you open the app, the AI reads your active tasks and generates a fresh, customized summary at the top of your Dashboard to help you focus.

---

## 📴 Offline Mode

Clarify is proudly **Offline-First**. 
This means you never need an internet connection to use the app. You can create, edit, complete, and delete tasks while entirely disconnected. Clarify remembers all your changes and handles the complex synchronization process in the background as soon as you have signal again.

## 🔐 Your Account & Privacy

- **Guest Mode**: All your data stays strictly on your physical device. Nothing is sent to the cloud.
- **Signed In**: Your data is securely synced to our encrypted cloud database. 
- **Privacy First**: The AI *only* accesses the specific notes or tasks necessary to answer your immediate questions. We do not use your personal notes to train global AI models.

## ❓ FAQ

**Can I use Clarify offline?**
Yes! You can use 100% of the core app (Notes, Tasks, Dashboard) completely offline. Features requiring the AI Assistant will naturally require an internet connection to process your request.

**Is my data private?**
Absolutely. Your data is secured with industry-standard database rules. You can only access your own data, and no one else can.

**Does the AI train on my notes?**
No. Your personal tasks and notes are used strictly as temporary context to answer your questions and are never used to train global AI models.

**Can I use it without signing in?**
Yes, the Guest Mode lets you use the app entirely locally without creating an account. 

---

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

**The AI says "I couldn't find any tasks."**
The AI searches the tasks currently in your Inbox. If your Inbox is empty, the AI won't have anything to search! Try creating a few tasks using the "+" button first.

**My tasks aren't syncing across devices.**
Make sure you are signed into the same Google Account on both devices. If you created tasks in Guest Mode, they remain local to that specific device.
